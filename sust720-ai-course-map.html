How to read this map

  1. Territory. Six stops, left to right, follow the river through the term. Each one has a learning goal and an assignment.
  2. People. Turn this on to see who's actually involved at each stop — it's never just "students."
  3. AI, boundaries & judgment. Three symbols: a teal dot marks where AI could help. A coral ring marks where AI is not allowed at all. A small violet diamond marks a decision that has to stay with a real person.
  4. Hand-offs. Everywhere an AI result gets checked by a person, and what happens if that AI result turns out to be wrong.
  5. Decision power. Who actually decides at each stop — both the official answer, and the quieter, less obvious one.
The five-minute test — after exploring, you should be able to answer two questions: where does AI touch this course, and whose call is it. If you can't yet, keep clicking stops.
